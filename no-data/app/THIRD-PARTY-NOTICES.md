# Bundled components

Ghostery adblocker 2.20.3 and its Electron/preload packages are unmodified and distributed under MPL-2.0. Source: https://github.com/ghostery/adblocker/tree/v2.20.3 . Package source maps and individual license files are included under node_modules. Dependency license files remain with their packages.

filters.bin is a build-time snapshot of Ghostery's public ads-and-tracking subscriptions and scriptlet resources, including EasyList, EasyPrivacy, Peter Lowe's list and uBlock Origin filters. Exact source lists, resources, copyright/license headers and source URLs are bundled in filter-sources. Upstream: https://github.com/ghostery/adblocker/tree/master/packages/adblocker/assets . Regenerate with build-filters.cjs. No telemetry or browsing data is included in this file. Filters are bundled program data, never a saved user profile.
